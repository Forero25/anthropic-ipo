Quarterly Trends Revenue Revenue increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented, from $376 million for the three months ended March 31, 2025 to $17.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026 . The increases in each quarter were driven by successive model and product releases that drove higher usage, expansion, and broader adoption across our offerings, with both consumption-based and subscription-based revenue contributing. The number of customers with $100,000 or more of revenue on a TTM basis grew from approximately 400 as of March 31, 2025 to more than 11,900 as of September 30, 2026 . For the three months ended September 30, 2025 to the three months ended December 31, 2025 , the increase in revenue due to higher usage was partially offset by a decrease of approximately $70 million from price reductions introduced as part of new model versions and contractual discounts. For the three months ended June 30, 2026 to the three months ended September 30, 2026 , the increase in revenue due to higher usage was partially offset by a decrease of approximately $600 million from price reductions introduced as part of new model versions and contractual discounts. Price changes did not have a material impact on the other quarterly revenue trends presented. 166 Table of Contents Operating Expenses Compute and Infrastructure Compute and infrastructure expenses increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented, from $944 million for the three months ended March 31, 2025 to $9.9 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026 , driven by the delivery of committed compute under our multi-year arrangements. Because we typically procure compute 12 to 18 months in advance of availability, these expenses are generally fixed in the near term and increase as committed compute becomes available to us. Compute and infrastructure expenses as a percentage of revenue decreased from 251% for the three months ended March 31, 2025 to 48% for the three months ended June 30, 2026 , as revenue growth outpaced the growth of our committed compute cost base, and increased to 58% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 , reflecting the delivery of additional compute. This includes $5.0 billion of compute under short-term commitments, which currently costs significantly more than comparable compute procured under our multi-year commitments. Personnel Personnel expenses increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented through the three months ended June 30, 2026 , driven by headcount growth and the associated increases in compensation, including stock- based compensation. Personnel expenses decreased in the three months ended September 30, 2026 , primarily driven by lower stock-based compensation expense due to certain option awards becoming fully vested, partially offset by higher cash compensation resulting from headcount growth. Bartz settlement In the three months ended September 30, 2025, we recorded a $1.5 billion charge to settle the Bartz litigation. See Note 13 to our consolidated financial statements included elsewhere in this prospectus. Sales, Marketing, and Partnerships Sales, marketing, and partnerships expenses increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented, with the most significant increases observed in the three months ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026 . The increases were primarily driven by fees paid to channel partners, reflecting the growth of our business, and increased marketing and advertising expenses, driven by higher media spend to support product launches. Sales, marketing, and partnerships expenses as a percentage of revenue decreased from 22% for the three months ended March 31, 2026 to 14% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 , primarily driven by a decrease in revenue generated from sales through channel partners as a percentage of total revenue and changes in the terms of our channel partner distribution arrangements. Other Operating and Administrative Other operating and administrative expenses increased over the quarters presented, other than in the three months ended March 31, 2026, which reflected lower donation matching program charges than in the three months ended December 31, 2025. The overall increase reflected vendor costs related to data quality and performance evaluation services, information technology and cloud software costs, payment processing and platform fees, and professional services costs as our business scaled. The increases in the three months ended December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2026 were primarily driven by non-cash charges related to our donation matching program. Our operating loss narrowed in the three months ended December 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026, and we generated operating income of $1.0 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026 , as revenue growth outpaced the growth in total operating expenses.
